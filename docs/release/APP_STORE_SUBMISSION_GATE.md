## Elevated App Review policy risks — resolve before spending on a company

- [ ] **Guideline 5.1.1(ix), individual developer vs. sensitive data — OPEN / HIGH RISK.** Apple says apps that require sensitive user information should be submitted by the legal entity that provides the service, not an individual. Blumi persists a chosen gender identity and discovery gender preferences; those matching fields could be treated as sensitive, particularly if Apple considers them to reveal romantic/sexual orientation. Apple's public guidance does not say whether this exact Blumi data model triggers the rule. Do not claim individual publication is safe, and do not register a company solely on this assumption. First ask Apple for app-specific guidance (see the draft below) and obtain Turkish legal/accounting advice before any entity setup. This is a risk assessment, not a legal conclusion.
- [ ] **Guideline 1.2, anonymous/random chat interpretation — OPEN / HIGH REVIEW RISK.** Blumi's intended flow is profile discovery → each person chooses → mutual match → text chat → optional invited room; phone OTP verifies an account, not a person's legal identity. Keep the product explanation accurate: pseudonymous profile, phone-verified account, no chat before mutual opt-in, no random one-tap connection. Demonstrate this exact flow and moderation controls in the submitted build. Do not merely rename or hide an anonymous/random experience to evade review. Apple can still decide that a product is anonymous/random chat; neither moderation features nor wording guarantees acceptance.

**Lowest-cost next move:** before company formation or a public submission, request a [Meet with Apple App Review appointment](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/810791) or contact Apple Developer Support, once the membership/account allows it. Ask specifically whether Blumi's phone-verified pseudonymous profiles, user-selected identity gender and discovery gender preferences trigger 5.1.1(ix), and whether a deliberate reciprocal match before any chat avoids the prohibited random/anonymous-chat characterization in 1.2. This conversation is guidance, not a pre-approval or guarantee. Save Apple's written answer to the release evidence.

Draft question for Apple (send privately through Apple's support/appointment flow; no credentials or real user records):

> Blumi is an 18+ social matching app. Users sign in using phone-number OTP, create pseudonymous profiles, choose profile/discovery preferences including gender fields, and can start durable text chat only after both users deliberately select each other and a mutual match is created. A shared text room can be entered only by invitation from that matched chat. The app does not pair users into a conversation at random and does not offer photos, live voice, video or paid digital purchases in the first release. The service stores account-linked profile/preferences and messages to provide matching, chat and safety controls. For App Review Guideline 5.1.1(ix), would this data model require the service to be submitted by an Apple Developer Program organization/legal entity rather than an individual developer? For Guideline 1.2, does this mutual-selection-before-chat model require any specific changes or evidence to distinguish it from random or anonymous chat? We are seeking guidance before incurring company-registration costs; we understand this is not a request for advance approval.

If Apple says an organization is required, its account must belong to the genuine legal entity that operates Blumi; a borrowed/nominee account or trade name is not a substitute. Apple says organizations need a recognized legal entity and D‑U‑N‑S Number, and that sole proprietorship/single-person businesses enroll as individuals. Apple permits an individual membership to be converted after the founder has established an eligible organization and supplies its D‑U‑N‑S details. These steps can create real tax, contract, privacy and operating responsibilities; they do not create “zero risk.” If Apple confirms that individual enrollment is acceptable for this product, continue with the individual account and its public seller-name implications. If the answer is ambiguous, keep this gate OPEN rather than spending or submitting on a guess.
