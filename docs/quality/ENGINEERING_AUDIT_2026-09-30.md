### Findings re-checked and closed without code change

These were reported by the initial exploration and rejected after evidence.
Their residual conditions stay tracked here.

- **RevenueCat webhook product mapping (not a bug).** The webhook reads
  `event.product_id`, which RevenueCat defines as the store product identifier
  ([webhook fields](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/integrations/webhooks/event-types-and-fields),
  checked 2026-09-30). Mobile purchases the same store identifiers
  (`apps/mobile/src/features/commerce/revenueCatCoinPackClient.ts` `COIN_PACKS`),
  and `revenueCatWebhook.test.ts` admits `com.blumi.mobile.coins.500`. Reconcile
  uses API v2 purchases, whose product reference is RevenueCat's own product
  entity (v2 `Product.id` such as `prod…` vs `store_identifier`), hence
  `REVENUECAT_COIN_PRODUCT_ID_MAP` (`revenueCatPurchaseVerifier.test.ts` maps
  `rc_product_500`). **Open conditions:** (1) App Store Connect product IDs must
  equal the canonical `com.blumi.mobile.coins.*` IDs; (2) the v2 Purchase
  `product_id` field definition was not found verbatim in the public v2 page —
  confirm with one sandbox purchase before enabling payments. Payments are off
  (`BLUMI_PAYMENTS_ENABLED=0`).
- **Logout / account switch data isolation (not a leak).** In-memory caches
  are keyed by owner and are not cleared on sign-out.
  `apps/mobile/src/features/session/accountSwitchIsolation.test.ts` proves
  another account cannot read the previous account's blocks, pending reports,
  saved connections (separate storage keys), or discovery query cache entries.
  **Open conditions:** per-account AsyncStorage data stays on the device after
  sign-out by design; a shared-device privacy decision (wipe on sign-out or
  not) is a product choice, not a defect.
- **Realtime event schema validation** — deferred to wave 2: events arrive
  from our authenticated server and handlers switch on known types.
- **Global request-schema enforcement** (`attachValidation` without a check) —
  deferred to wave 2 with staging verification; a global 400 could break
  clients that send extra fields, and handlers already parse with zod.
- **Realtime per-event re-authorisation cost** — deferred; caching changes
  revocation latency and needs measurement first.
